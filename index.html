A note is not a sentence.
It is four facts and a tap.

Pick the type in the sidebar, tap the line, pick the cast member. The note stores what kind of note it is, who it belongs to, what page it was on, and which run it came from — which is exactly what lets the end-of-run report split itself by actor without anyone re-reading it.

The seven note types

Skip
An actor skipped a line.
Para
An actor paraphrased a line.
Called
An actor called for a line.
Add
An actor added a line, or words that are not in the script.
Gen
A general note that is none of the above.
Jumped
An actor jumped ahead to later in the script.
Missed
An actor missed a line, or words inside one.

Price

A per-seat tool punishes you for inviting the director. This one does not: the price is the same with four people in the session or forty.

Buying for a department, a university, or a company in rep? Enterprise is priced per company and set up around how your season actually runs.

Asked at every load-in.

Will it work on the house Mac mini in the booth?
Anything running a recent Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox. The interface is built dark for a dim booth, and there is nothing to install.
How do my ASMs and director get in?
Every production has a join code. Share it with the company and they are in — there is no per-seat licence and no extra charge per person.
What about scanned PDFs from a stage rental?
Line Notes needs a PDF with a selectable text layer. If your script was scanned from a physical copy, run it through OCR — Adobe Acrobat will do it — before uploading. You can also draw line zones by hand in the Script Editor.
Why not just use a shared doc?
A note here is anchored to a line, an actor, a session, and a type. That structure is what lets the report group itself by actor, without anyone reading the document back through.
Can I get my data out?
Every run produces a report you can print, save as a PDF, or send by email. Past run reports stay listed in the production and can be reopened any time.
